After a discreet and thorough search the services where procured of a hunter specicialising in big game troubling remote mining operations in unstable parts of the world and a former special operations K9 combat medic to perform a mysterious mission on the remote Atlantic island of Isla Corona del Raptor.

On arrival to the island it was clear that there was serious issues with "broken" high volatage perimiter fences and signs of attacks on the local wildlife. After being taken to the hatchery on the north side of the island to be briefed on the hunting operation a flash of purple light was seen followed by a loss of power. Going outside a purple cloud could be seen over the centre of the island and the remaining operational electronics going haywire.
